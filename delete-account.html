Delete Your Account and Data

Applies to the Babil mobile application. Last updated: June 1, 2026

You can permanently delete your Babil account and all associated personal and financial data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what may be retained.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

Open Babil and go to Profile → Delete Account. After you confirm, your account and data are permanently and immediately deleted from our systems. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@babilfinance.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line Delete my account. We will verify your request and delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • All financial records you entered: expenses, incomes, accounts, transfers, balances, and daily work logs.
  • Your profile information: first/last name and age range.
  • Your email address and account credentials.

What may be retained

Records of your purchases and subscription status (held by us, by our subscription provider RevenueCat, and by the app stores) may be retained for a limited period as required to provide the service and to meet legal, tax, and app-store record-keeping obligations, even after your account is deleted.

Active subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Auto-renewable subscriptions continue under your Google or Apple account until you cancel them in the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Please cancel there to stop future charges.

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: support@babilfinance.com
